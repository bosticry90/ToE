## Purpose and boundary

Gravity from Entropy (GfE) is registered as a direct comparator for the ToE
master-action, statistical-sector, seam-admissibility, emergence, and
gravity-cosmology programs. It supplies a concrete published example in which a
quantum-relative-entropy relation between physical and matter-induced geometry is
placed in a gravitational action.

This entry does not validate the ToE, adopt GfE, replace the present gravitational
sector, promote any `C_k` constraint into dynamics, remove or repair the speculative
statistical term, close a pillar or seam, or establish empirical support. It does not
open a GfE execution lane. A later comparator requires a separate bounded selector.

## Source-supported comparator summary

The published foundational paper treats the Lorentzian metric as a local
operator-like object, constructs a second metric induced by matter fields, and
postulates a quantum-relative-entropy action between them. It reports modified
second-order gravitational equations, an Einstein limit at low coupling, and a
G-field-dependent effective cosmological term.

The accepted thermodynamics paper extends the proposal to homogeneous and isotropic
FRW settings. It associates local metric degrees of freedom with effective
temperatures and pressures satisfying a first-law relation in the stated regime. It
reports non-increasing GQRE per unit volume alongside non-decreasing total entropy.

These are claims and derivations within GfE, not findings about the ToE. The present
cosmology treatment is not a derivation of inhomogeneous structure formation and does
not by itself predict galaxies, perturbation growth, CMB anisotropies, lensing, or
nonlinear collapse.
