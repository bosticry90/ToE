- No task may depend for completion on private, access-controlled, or restricted
  evidence.
- No task may wait on a third party to provide evidence or cooperation.
- Public papers, public repositories, and openly available data remain usable.
- Internal theory calculations, simulations, synthetic injections, and
  sensitivity forecasts remain usable when their non-empirical status is explicit.
- This policy may be reopened only by a later explicit user instruction.
