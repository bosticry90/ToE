Non-claim boundary:
- This audit does not promote the candidate master action.
- This audit does not claim full document parsing.
- This audit does not claim global ToE closure, seam closure, empirical validation, gauge recovery, Standard Model recovery, or interacting-QFT completion.
- This audit is a strict-physics fidelity check for the bounded free-scalar regime only.

## Classification Key

`mechanically checked`:
- The relation is represented by Lean definitions and theorems.
- The result is checked by the Lean build.

`manually curated but checkable`:
- The mapping from prose/math text to Lean token is manually selected.
- Once selected, the token class, disposition, coefficient, or zero block is checkable in Lean.

`retained assumption`:
- The relation depends on a physics convention, regime selection, prose interpretation, or text-to-Lean fidelity step not mechanically derived from the Markdown source.
