## Important supporting-evidence discovery

The acquired CC BY dissertation, *A Fourier-Bessel Test of the Gravitational
Inverse-Square Law* by J. G. Lee, contains materially useful supporting
evidence:

- Appendix A prints 95 run identifiers, one separation with uncertainty per
  run, and the 120, 18, and 54 omega torque values with pointwise
  uncertainties.
- Chapter 11 identifies the five fitted nuisance quantities as `x0`, `y0`,
  `s0`, the surface-overcut parameter, and the calibration scale `gamma`, and
  prints their measured central values and widths.
- It gives the chi-square form, the Fourier-Bessel calculation structure,
  correction terms, calibration discussion, and the published limit rule.

The accepted packet classifies the ResearchWorks dissertation as supporting
methods only, not a primary numerical substitute. Its exact printed tables
therefore improve the custody map but do not advance a primary inventory item
