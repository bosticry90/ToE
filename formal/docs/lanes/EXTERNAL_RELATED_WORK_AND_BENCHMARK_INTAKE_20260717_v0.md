- `RELATED_WORK_CASE_STUDY_NONCLAIM`

Primary source pins:
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30402
- Source status: `PRIMARY_PREPRINT_PINNED`

Source-supported observation:
- Across nine FLiBe clusters, the heterogeneous quantum-classical workflow reproduced
  fragment ground-state energies within `0.7 kcal/mol` of full configuration interaction,
  with mean absolute deviation `0.3 kcal/mol`.
- Fragmented versus unfragmented conformational-energy and tritium-binding-energy
  differences were about `12 kcal/mol` and `110 kcal/mol` on average.
- The authors therefore identify fragment construction, rather than fragment solution,
  as the dominant algorithmic bias in this bounded workflow.

  embedding, or reconstruction semantics are inadequate.
- This is a mainstream computational example of pillar competence failing to guarantee
  seam admissibility.
