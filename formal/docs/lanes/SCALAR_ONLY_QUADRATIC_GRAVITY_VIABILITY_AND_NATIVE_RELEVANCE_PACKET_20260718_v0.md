## Purpose and provenance

The accepted quadratic comparison found that the local Ricci-squared direction
contains an additional massive spin-2 pole with negative conserved-source-
saturated residue under the frozen Minkowski conventions. The response selector
therefore chose the remaining modified comparison subfamily

\[
S_g^{\rm cmp}=\frac{c^3}{16\pi G}\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}
\left(R+\alpha R^2\right),
\qquad \beta=0,
\]

for a viability and native-relevance investigation.

This is a research-priority choice, not a theory choice. The restriction
`beta=0` is conditionally motivated by a supplied standard ghost-avoidance
criterion. It is not an adopted ToE law. The action is not ToE-native, is not a
candidate master action, and cannot be promoted by success in this lane.

The packet keeps two questions separate:

1. **Viability:** is this supplied comparison subfamily coherent over a bounded
   parameter and background domain?
2. **Native relevance:** does an accepted project object have a complete
   mathematical bridge to the gravitational scalar?

Viability does not imply native relevance. Native resemblance does not imply a
bridge.
