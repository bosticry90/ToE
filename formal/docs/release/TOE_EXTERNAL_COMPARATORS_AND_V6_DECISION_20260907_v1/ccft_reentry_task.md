# First Astra CCFT re-entry task — one candidate, one recovery

Status: `DRAFT_NOT_LAUNCHED__SEPARATE_ROUTE_AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED`.
Target model, if/when authorized: `gpt-6-astra`.

## Existing boundary

The [post-CCFT route decision](../POST_BROADER_MULTIFIELD_CCFT_REOPENING_BOUNDED_EVIDENTIARY_PASS_SCIENTIFIC_ROUTE_SELECTION_20260809_v0.md)
describes the historical family as source-enriched but formulation-underdefined:
five unresolved conflicts, no mutually consistent canonical core, and no
automatic archive retry or CCFT-v1 synthesis. A model upgrade is not new
scientific evidence that removes that boundary.

This brief designs a possible later task; it does not open archives, select an
ontology, supply a new master action, or declare that coherent CCFT exists.
The proposed order is bounded VPC adjudication, relevant SU(5) scientific work
and its observable endpoint, then explicit native route selection. A later
owner decision can select a different schedule, but must say so rather than
silently treating this draft as launch authority.

## Preconditions to launch

- Named route-selection authority permitting either historical recovery on
  genuinely new evidence, or **new candidate construction with new provenance**.
  The two are not interchangeable; no relabeling invention as recovered history.
- A curated local source manifest with exact hashes and locators, including
  current authority, the preserved conflict inventory, surviving formulations,
  definitions/actions, prior falsifiers and the selected known-model comparator.
  Do not ingest the entire workspace or start an open-ended archive search.
- A bounded resource/time budget and one output target, chosen before derivation.
  No inference of an unlimited search budget from the million-token context.
- An available verification route for the selected mathematics; missing VPC
  capability is an explicit dependency, never an excuse for model-certified PASS.

## Execution prompt to use only after those preconditions

> Work from the authorized, hash-bound native source corpus. Construct at most
> one minimal mutually consistent candidate, or demonstrate the precise
> underdefinition or contradiction that prevents one. Begin by reconciling
> symbol meanings, field/state spaces, dimensions, domains, symmetry actions
> and the five preserved formulation conflicts. Mark each retained statement
> as inherited, derived, independently sourced, or newly proposed. Do not merge
> incompatible historical variants by changing meanings silently.
>
> For a viable candidate, specify an executable action or dynamics, boundary
> conditions, observables and the selected approximation regime. Derive the
> equations of motion and constraints; test the relevant stability, consistency
> and symmetry conditions. Identify its closest established-model comparator
> and distinguish an exact representation change from a new physical prediction.
>
> Complete one vertical recovery calculation. The first preferred target, only
> if the candidate actually supplies matter and weak-gravity dynamics, is the
> ideal quantum-free-fall phase and agreement between laboratory and falling
> frame descriptions. Use the already defined apparatus and timing convention;
> derive gravitational coupling and the frame phase instead of inserting the
> expected coefficient. If the candidate cannot supply those inputs, report
> the exact missing map. Do not switch to an easier target after a failure
> without renewed scope selection.
>
> Emit declarative candidate evidence and independently testable intermediate
> obligations. Keep proposal code outside the verifier. Preserve failed attacks,
> unsupported operations and non-derived assumptions. Then identify one
> candidate-forced observable discriminator from the comparator, with units,
> domain, uncertainty and a route to data. If the formulations are equivalent
> or the parameter is freely fitted, report no earned discriminator.
>
> Stop after the one recovery/discriminator assessment or a concrete failure.
> Do not build another governance framework, expand the benchmark library,
> change authority, reopen production, or invent missing empirical inputs.

## Required deliverables and stopping states

| Deliverable | Minimum acceptance |
| --- | --- |
| Candidate/source map | Every assumption and term has a locator or explicitly new provenance; no unresolved symbol-role collision |
| Conflict disposition | All preserved conflicts explicitly resolved within the selected candidate, or an evidence-linked blocker |
| Executable core | Defined states/fields, units, dynamics, boundary conditions, constraints and scope; no merely schematic action |
| Recovery packet | One source-to-observable derivation and independent calculation; per-output verification bounded to supported semantics |
| Discriminator report | Derived rather than fitted difference, or honest equivalence/no-discriminator result; no forced novelty |
| Closeout | Sources, outputs, limitations, attacks, replay evidence and exactly what remains unearned |

Permitted outcomes: `CANDIDATE_WITH_BOUNDED_RECOVERY_EVIDENCE`,
`REPRESENTATION_EQUIVALENT_NO_NEW_PHYSICS`, `UNDERDEFINED`,
`INCONSISTENT_IN_DECLARED_SCOPE`, `RECOVERY_FAILED`, or
`VERIFICATION_CAPABILITY_MISSING`. These are task-report labels, not additions
to VPC or scientific-authority enums. A negative outcome can complete the task.

The other three comparators remain deferred, not automatic CCFT ingredients.
Neither Tsallis statistics, dark photons, a material substrate nor emergent
spacetime may be selected solely because its comparator was interesting.
