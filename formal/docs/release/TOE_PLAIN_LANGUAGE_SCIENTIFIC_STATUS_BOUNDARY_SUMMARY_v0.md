## Plain-Language Summary

The project has not proven a completed Theory of Everything, and the repo's own controls prevent that interpretation.

The project has not discovered or confirmed new physics yet. It has not produced a new experimentally confirmed law of nature, a new accepted empirical prediction, or a final master equation from which all known physics has been derived.

The strongest result so far is different:

```text
The project has built a rigorous framework for testing whether known physics can be connected through meaning-preserving bridges.
```

A meaning-preserving bridge is a rule that does more than make two equations look similar. It must preserve what the objects mean, how they behave, what assumptions they need, and what claims they are allowed to support.

The novel part is architectural, not yet physical.

## What Is Mostly Known Physics

Much of the physics content currently used by the repo comes from known physics families:

```text
action principles
Euler-Lagrange variation
free scalar-field equations
Klein-Gordon-style residuals
Maxwell/U(1)-style electromagnetism
quantum state evolution
entropy and probability scaffolds
weak-field and Poisson-style gravity limits
semiclassical-looking stress-energy source maps
```

Using these ingredients is not a weakness by itself. A unification project has to recover known physics. But recovering or reorganizing known physics is not the same as discovering new physics.

## What May Be Novel

The project's strongest possible novelty is methodological and architectural:

```text
strict nonclaim governance
meaning-preserving bridge standards
source-map witness-chain discipline
formal dependency tracking
obstruction mapping
false-closure prevention
```

This means the repo is trying to stop a common mistake: treating a local equation match, a zero residual, or a useful analogy as if it were already a complete physical unification.

That is valuable. But it is not the same as a new experimentally confirmed theory of nature.

## Lean And Math Boundary

The Lean layer contains real bounded mathematics. Some files prove actual formal statements inside Lean.

The Lean layer also contains release/control markers. These markers may typecheck as Lean theorems, but some are intentionally simple status records rather than physics derivations.

So the safe reading is:

```text
green Lean build = the included formal statements typecheck under their assumptions
green Lean build != completed physical theory
green Lean build != empirical validation
green Lean build != source-map or seam closure unless the specific authoritative surface says so
```

The repo currently has no active `sorry` or `admit` proof holes in the audited Lean ledger, but it still has retained axioms and spec-backed assumptions. Those assumptions matter. A theorem proved under supplied assumptions does not automatically prove that nature satisfies those assumptions.

## QFT-GR And Source-Map Boundary

QFT-GR remains the decisive hard seam.

The repo may register control-plane progress on source-map status, but that must not be confused with full QFT-GR seam closure or empirical validation.

The important scientific question is:

```text
Can the quantum stress-energy side be turned into a valid gravity source with the required conservation, renormalization, Bianchi compatibility, Einstein-coupling, weak-field, and recovery witnesses?
```

Until those requirements are met at the needed authority level, the safe public statement remains:
